# Product vision

Source: 01 BRD, Section 1.


## 1.1 The problem

When a patient needs blood, the family usually starts a search on its own: phone calls to relatives and acquaintances, appeals on social media that carry personal phone numbers, and messages forwarded through informal networks. The same few donors are called many times for the same patient, appeals continue to circulate after the need has passed, donor phone numbers become public, and nobody owns the follow-through. Most importantly, families are often told that "a donor was found" when no donation has happened yet, which creates false assurance at the most critical moment. The Organization has no reliable view of demand, response or outcome.


## 1.2 The solution

The Brur Blood Donation Application (BBDA) is a single mobile app for Android and iOS with role-based navigation for five roles: Donor, Patient or Patient Representative, Support, Moderation and Organization Administrator. Users register with a Bangladesh mobile number verified by SMS OTP and sign in with phone number and password, with SMS OTP as a fallback for public users and as a second step for staff. A patient or representative submits a request; Support verifies it before any donor is contacted; the system searches consenting donors and recommends whom to invite; donors answer privately; the first willing donor opens a Coordination Case owned by one Support agent, who arranges attendance and obtains a sourced outcome; the patient receives verified progress throughout; and the case closes with follow-up. If nobody responds, or a donor withdraws or does not attend, the system returns to the search and keeps the patient informed. Every important action is recorded in a tamper-evident audit log and a status history; Support and Administrators work with an approximate donor map, and a donor's exact location or phone number is revealed only for an active request the staff member is assigned to or oversees, with a recorded reason.


## 1.3 Business value

Faster, organized help, with one accountable Support owner per case.

Honest progress: four separate milestones prevent false assurance.

Donor protection: consent-based, limited, private outreach; no public phone numbers.

Trust and safety: verification before outreach, moderation and pause controls.

Organizational visibility: reports on demand, response, attendance, confirmed outcomes and service levels.


## 1.4 What BBDA is not — the clinical boundary

BBDA is a coordination and communication tool. It does not perform clinical screening, does not declare blood compatible, does not release blood for transfusion, and does not manage blood inventory, laboratory testing or unit recall. Donor matching in BBDA is an outreach recommendation ("who we contact"), never a clinical compatibility decision. The provisional next contact date shown to donors is an outreach-timing estimate only; donation suitability is assessed by the relevant blood service under its own selection criteria. Hospitals and collection services remain external parties and are not app users.
